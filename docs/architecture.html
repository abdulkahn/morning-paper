Remembers what you read. Opening a story writes story_reads. Tomorrow, facts published or learned after that moment come back as "Since you last read", in the updates column and on the timeline.
Remembers what you know. Concepts you've met are tracked in reader_concepts; explainers appear only for terms that are new to you, and Three Things quizzes you on yesterday's facts.
Frozen, honest editions. Each day's paper is built once and saved; web, archive, audio and PDF all render that same snapshot, so a past paper never changes.
